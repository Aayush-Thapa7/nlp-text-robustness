What are we testing?

Small character-level changes can affect how a text classifier represents words and predicts sentiment.

Deletion — remove characters
Insertion — add characters
Substitution — replace characters
Transposition — swap neighboring characters

How to read the result

Compare the original and corrupted predictions. A changed label indicates that this input's predicted sentiment was sensitive to the selected corruption.

Experiment note

This interactive demonstration tests one review at a time. It does not replace aggregate evaluation across a test dataset.
